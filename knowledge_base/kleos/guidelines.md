# Kléos — Brand Guidelines

> **Industry:** Food, Drinks & Beverages (FMCG / premium spirits)
> **Archetype:** Premium Greek spirit (barrel-aged, modern-classic)
> **One line:** Old soul. New fire.

*("Kléos" / κλέος is the ancient Greek word for glory and renown earned, not given.)*

---

## 1. Story

Kléos is a barrel-aged Greek spirit built for a generation that wants the
craft and story of a heritage drink without the dusty, gatekept rituals around
it. Distilled the old way, poured the new way — neat by the fire or long over
ice in a loud bar. It carries 3,000 years of Greek glory-seeking on its
shoulders and wears it lightly. Kléos is for people who make their own myths.

## 2. Mission

To bottle Greek fire and hand it to a new generation — proudly, playfully, and
without the rulebook.

## 3. Values

- **Earned glory.** Kléos is renown you work for. Substance behind the swagger.
- **Heritage, not nostalgia.** We honour the old craft; we don't live in a museum.
- **Boldness with taste.** Confident, never crass.
- **Conviviality.** The drink is the excuse; the people are the point.

## 4. Strategic positioning

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Premium / craft spirits |
| **Audience** | 25–40 urban "modern classicists" — value story, craft, design |
| **Competitive frame** | Vs. safe heritage brands (too stuffy) and trend spirits (no soul) |
| **Price posture** | Premium and proud; never on deep discount |
| **Promise** | A drink with a story worth retelling. |

## 5. Tone of voice

Kléos is **bold, witty, proud, and sensory** — a charismatic friend with great
taste and a sharp line. Heritage swagger meets modern bite. We use myth and fire
imagery, short punchy lines, and confident wordplay. We never explain the joke.

**Voice in three words:** Bold · Witty · Proud

### Do's
- Be confident and a little cheeky. Earn the swagger with craft detail.
- Use fire, gold, myth, glory and heritage imagery — modernised, not museum-y.
- Short, punchy, rhythmic lines. Wordplay and a wink are welcome.
- Reference the craft (barrel, distill, age, copper, oak) to back up the boast.
- Talk to a savvy, in-the-know audience as a peer.

### Don'ts
- Never stuffy, formal, or "heritage-museum" dull.
- Never crass, laddish, or about getting drunk. Glory ≠ excess.
- **Responsible marketing (non-negotiable):** never target or depict under-25s,
  never link alcohol to social/sexual success, driving, or coping with problems,
  never encourage excessive or rapid drinking.
- Always include "Enjoy responsibly" / "Drink responsibly. 18+." on campaign copy.
- No tired spirits clichés ("smooth," "premium taste," "perfect serve," "crafted
  to perfection," "like no other").
- Don't over-explain. Confidence doesn't justify itself.

## 6. Lexicon

**Reach for:** fire, gold, glory, myth, oak, barrel, copper, age, ember, neat,
toast, legend, renown, slow-aged, Greek, bold, raise.

**Avoid:** smooth, premium taste, perfect serve, crafted to perfection, smooth
finish, like no other, sophisticated palate, "treat yourself," wasted, hammered,
shots (in the excess sense), getting drunk.
