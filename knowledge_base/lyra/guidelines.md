# Lyrá — Brand Guidelines

> **Industry:** Travel, Hospitality & Destinations
> **Archetype:** Luxury coastal resort & residences (Cyclades, Greece)
> **One line:** A place that does less, so you can feel more.

---

## 1. Story

Lyrá is a 38-key resort carved into a south-facing cliff on a quiet Cycladic
island. It is named after the lyre — and after Lyra, the constellation you can
still see here because there is almost no light pollution. The brand was built
against the "more is more" reflex of luxury travel: no buffets, no animation
teams, no gold taps. Luxury at Lyrá is *subtraction* — space, silence, salt air,
and time that moves at the speed of the sea.

## 2. Mission

To give guests back the rarest luxury of all: their own attention.

## 3. Values

- **Stillness over spectacle.** We design for calm, not for the feed.
- **Craft over flash.** Local stone, hand-thrown ceramics, slow food.
- **Discretion.** We never name-drop guests, never chase celebrity.
- **Place before brand.** The island is the hero; we are its host.

## 4. Strategic positioning

| | |

| **Category** | Ultra-premium experiential hospitality |

## 5. Tone of voice

Lyrá speaks the way the place feels: **unhurried, sensory, understated, sure of
itself.** Think a well-read host who doesn't need to raise their voice. Short
sentences. Long pauses. Concrete nouns over adjectives. The sea, the stone, the
light do the selling — we just point.

**Voice in three words:** Serene · Sensory · Assured

### Do's
- Lead with the senses: light, salt, stone, shade, water, hush.
- Use short, declarative sentences. Let white space breathe.
- Show luxury through specificity (the ceramic, the cellar, the 6am swim), never
  by claiming it.
- Speak in calm second person or quiet first-person-plural ("we").
- Be confident enough to understate.

### Don'ts
- Never use the word "luxury," "luxurious," "exclusive," or "world-class." Show, don't label.
- No hype or urgency: no "book now," "don't miss," "amazing," "stunning," "unforgettable."
- No exclamation marks. Ever.
- No emoji.
- No superlatives ("best," "finest," "most"). No stock-travel clichés
  ("hidden gem," "paradise," "bucket list," "home away from home").
- Never mention discounts, deals, or offers.

### Signature rhythm
> The day starts when you decide it does.
> A swim before the island wakes. Bread still warm. The sea, unhurried.

## 6. Lexicon

**Reach for:** light, salt, stone, shade, horizon, stillness, hush, ritual,
slow, linen, ceramic, cellar, cliff, swim, breath, time.

**Avoid:** luxury, exclusive, premium, world-class, stunning, amazing, paradise,
hidden gem, escape (overused), pamper, indulge, lavish, opulent.
