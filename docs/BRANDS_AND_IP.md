# A note on the brands and IP

All three brands in this prototype (Lyrá, Anása, and Kléos) are fictional, created for this exercise. Their guidelines, voice rules, and "past campaigns" are synthesized to be realistic and to cover three very different voice and compliance profiles across three of INTERWEAVE's industries (Travel & Hospitality, Public Sector / Social Causes, Food & Drinks).

They are archetype-inspired by the kind of work in INTERWEAVE's public portfolio (resort branding, public-good campaigns, premium Greek drinks), but no real client data, confidential material, or proprietary brand book was used. The "campaign outcomes" are illustrative, not real metrics.

This is intentional, per the brief: "you can synthesize these from publicly available material" and "one fictional or real Interweave-style brand of your choice."

For a real deployment, each brand directory would be populated from the client's actual brand book and campaign history, with strategist sign-off. See `docs/BUILD.md` under "What I'd need to productionize this."
